# Changelog

All notable changes to Sidera. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org).

## 0.2.0 — Sidera for iPad, rebuilt for writing

- **All your boards on the iPad.** Switch the iPad connection on once (sidebar → iPad, Settings → iPad or ⇧⌘D); the
  iPad opens on a live overview of every whiteboard, opens any of them and reopens the last one after a reconnect.
  The per-board iPad button and the top bar on the iPad are gone — just the board name and undo / redo float on the board.
- **Handwriting with Apple Pencil**: much smoother strokes (denser Pencil samples, no stall after each stroke), palm
  rejection, no selection box or connection points popping up while writing; the iPad opens in the pen tool and the
  pen bar only shows there.
- **New pen bar** (Mac and iPad): pen, marker in three widths, laser pointer, eraser and lasso (circle ink to move or
  delete it, with undo); recent colours plus a 36-colour palette and a custom colour; drag the bar anywhere.
- The pen tool sits right after the cursor in the toolbar.

## 0.1.0 — first public release

- Whiteboards with text boxes, tables, sixteen chart types (also linked to tables), images & PDFs, sections,
  connectors, mindmaps, Mermaid diagrams, link previews and live TradingView stock charts.
- Pen mode with pen, highlighter and eraser; Sidera for iPad with live, end-to-end encrypted board sync and Apple Pencil.
- Card library with backlinks, tags, kanban and full-text search; tasks; PDF reader with highlights.
- In-app AI assistant (bring your own key) and an MCP server with 50+ tools for Claude Desktop, Claude Code and others.
- Local accounts and guest mode; English and German.
